# Viewing profiles

- Who’s watching appears after sign-in, including restored accounts and deep links.
- Up to three profiles, editable names, and eight original avatar colors.
- Profile 1 uses your original account history. Extra profiles use separate Jellyfin playback identities.
- Switch profile is available from the account menu. Playback stops and reports drain before switching.
- Managed tokens stay in memory; the saved account remains the original login.
- Requires SpeshTV Core 3.1.0.9 or newer. Older servers offer the original-account compatibility mode.

This is a test candidate. Physical Roku verification of switching, playback progress, and remote focus is pending.
